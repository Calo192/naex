Design proposal · pravidlá v12.7 · 20 Nov 2026

Väčšie kombá, menej účtovníctva

NA EX! already has a combo engine most card games would envy: the spoj, NA EX that turns a card inside out, and Nástroje slid under cards. It can put ×8 on a single card today. In 358 battles played by your bots it never did. The game does not need a second combo system. It needs the one it has to be visible on the table, reachable on purpose, and free of the exceptions that make players stop calculating.

Legal under v12.7 and evaluated by your own evaluateLine: Veterán, Silná nezávislá žena, NA EX and Energeťák. Four actions at ×8, 32 tuhé and 8 cigarettes per Pitka from one card. Step through it under Kombá.

What 60 bot games say

I added a telemetry tool, tools/bot/komba.ts, that plays bot against bot (stredná, all four povahy, fixed seeds) and measures the line at every battle: 60 games, 358 battles, 9,955 action slots. Bots do not plan combos, so read these numbers as the floor a player gets without trying. Your AI playtesters report bigger ones (Hasič with Recidivista, Výtržník with Vidly for 12 tuhé), but they also say they cannot compute them without the náhľad.

Where combos die

  • Modifiers meet by accident. Take two random cards of one owner: Posilnenie faces Posilnenie on 0.2 % of joints, Odpor faces Odpor on 0.9 %. Posilnenie is printed on the left edge of 8 of the 10 cards that carry it, so two of your own cards almost never line it up.
  • Lines are short. Seven cards lie in the line at battle, three and a half per player, and 60 % of runs are a single card. A three-card combo is the practical maximum.
  • Factions are thin. In 75 % of battles a player's largest faction is two cards or fewer. Spoločník finds no partner or one partner 90 % of the time, and Influencer, Pracujúci and Závislý are read by no card at all.

What limits the fix

  • The engine persists little. 0.3 Nástroje per player in Dedičstvo at battle; a Nástroj under a card in 17 % of battles.
  • The leader already runs away. Bots finish 41 : 16 on average; the playtests score Rovnováha 5.3 and name snowball first. More power cannot simply mean more tuhé for whoever is ahead.
  • Nobody can read a spoj. Nine of fourteen November ratings complain that the result of a joint cannot be worked out without the náhľad. Hĺbka vs. zložitosť fell from 7.0 to 6.2.

Four rules I held myself to

  • A combo must be visible before it is counted. If it does not show as a badge on a card, it does not exist for the player.
  • Combos pay in tempo and options first. Poldeci, cards and cigarettes spread out; raw tuhé feeds the snowball.
  • One exception out for every rule in. The package below adds one rule (Štrng) and two card keywords (Repete, Vsuvka) and removes two exceptions and seven evaluation steps.
  • No caps, and the opponent can always answer on the line. Podstrčenie, NA EX and Dopiť za teba stay the counterplay. Interakcia is your 8.7.

K1 · Otoč a zdvoj

K1Streamline · no change to results

Vzbura, Forsáž, posilnená Vzbura, NA EX, the NA EX state from Vínko: these are five names for combinations of two things a card can be. Flipped, or doubled. Teach and draw the modifier system as two badges and the outcome of a spoj becomes something a player reads off the card.

Rule text

Karta môže niesť dva odznaky. ↻ Naopak: nespojené akcie sú aktívne, spojené nie. ×2 Zdvoj: aktívne akcie sa rátajú dvakrát. Odznaky sa skladajú: dva ↻ sa zrušia, dve ×2 dajú ×4.

Try it on a real line

Recidivista lies on Snúbenica at the Krčma. His two Hazardy are joined, his Petelica and Rana face the open end. Add badges and watch what the engine computes.

Evidence

  • I wrote the two-badge model as 80 lines of code and compared it with evaluateLine on 4,870 random lines: 77,902 action slots, 98.35 % identical. On lines without NA EX the match is 100 %.
  • Every remaining difference is one of three NA EX special cases, listed on the right. Nothing else in §6 is needed to predict a line.
  • Playtest 20261117: “Počet výnimiek (Odpor a Posilnenie pod NA EX, posun rečí, reťaz premien) je na hranici; bez náhľadu sa nedá hrať.”

The three exceptions

  • Keep: an unjoined Odpor shields the card from NA EX. It is your decision of 11 Nov, it is one sentence, and it creates play.
  • Drop: an unjoined Posilnenie “podrží” NA EX. It touched 214 of 77,902 slots and no player predicts it.
  • Replace: “NA EX cannot go on a card with Vzbura” becomes the composition rule. Two ↻ cancel, ×2 stays. The engine already evaluates it that way, as the Vidly-plus-NA EX case above shows.

What it buys

  • The ten-step evaluation order of §6 becomes three: Premeň, Spoj, Odznaky.
  • Stacking becomes legible. ×4 and ×8 are a number on the card, visible to both players before the Pitka.
  • Nine card texts get shorter (see Škrty).

Cost and risk

  • Client: two badges on the card and in the náhľad. Pravidlá §6 rewritten around them. Engine untouched if both exceptions stay.
  • Dropping “podrží” and the Vzbura ban changes a handful of outcomes; a rozhodca pass over §6–§7 tests covers it.
  • Vzbura and Forsáž keep their names as flavour for ↻ and ×2 from a spoj.

K2 · Štrng

K2New rule · one sentence

Today nothing rewards a beautiful joint except the actions on it, and a third icon lining up is worth the same as the second. Štrng makes the perfect fit an event and pays for it in tempo: the card that clinks costs you nothing.

Rule text

Štrng: keď karta, ktorú vyložíš lícom hore, vytvorí so susednou kartou tri páry, dolej si jeden vypitý poldeci.

Measured in the engine

How to read that

  • It is safe. Five percent more cards, two percent longer, no movement in the score gap.
  • It is small on its own. Štrng does not create fireworks; it gives every placement a second question (can I make the third pair?) and gives cards a hook to hang effects on. The fireworks come from Družba, Harmonikár and Štamperlík, which the bots cannot play yet.
  • Three pairs is the right bar. 4.7 % of card-to-card joints reach it. Two pairs or more is half of all joints: every other card free.
  • Cards only. 18.5 % of card-to-location joints are already triple, because level-3 and pinnacle edges carry three or four actions; the first card to the lokalita would often be free.

Alternatives

  • A · poldeci back (recommended). Poldeci is every resource at once: another card, a NA EX, or a card at the pas.
  • B · draw a card. Weaker tempo, more luck of the draw, and it stacks with pas za karty.
  • C · a cigarette. Feeds the economy that already wins: Ekonóm went 3 : 0 in the last styles playtest.

Risk

  • A face-down card (Záverečná, odpoveď na NA EX) must not Štrng, or the refund leaks what it is.
  • The variant lottery matters more. That is the point, but the ponuka should show the layout clearly.

K3 and K4 · Two changes in the card set, no new rules

K3Parta · Spoločník counts everyone

Rule text

Spoločník: Tag — akcie z pečate máš za každú inú svoju odkrytú kartu s tým tagom.

Today Spoločník pays once, however many tag-mates you field, so a second Pamätník is worth nothing to Veštica. Counting each one gives every faction tag a reason to be drafted and gives the deck an identity that survives from round to round, which the line itself cannot. In bot lines Spoločník is active 70 % of the time but averages 0.8 partners, so the cards would be re-priced rather than doubled: Veštica one Povera per Pamätník instead of two once.

Why this one

  • It is the cheapest way to make a combo span more than two cards: the payoff no longer needs adjacency.
  • It feeds Klebetník, your weakest style (2 : 13 over all style playtests), through Veštica and Šarlatán.
  • Nepriateľ stays binary. Counting the opponent's cards would punish them for playing.

What it needs

  • The ponuka must show how many cards of each faction you own, or nobody drafts for it.
  • Two or three cards with two faction tags (Brigádnik below) to raise density.
  • A check against stomp: Kočovník at three Pecky is 6 tuhé from one pečať.
K4Posilnenie doprava · data only

Seven cards that make the engine turn

Six Občania and one Nástroj, each a single job in a combo. One new keyword, Repete, and one new placement, Vsuvka; everything else uses triggers you already have. Edges are fixed layouts so the examples are exact; variants would be generated as usual. The hatched panel is a brief for the ilustrátor.

Four combos, step by step

Each line below is evaluated live by the game's own core. The first is legal today. The other three show what the proposals add; numbers the core does not know are computed by the page and marked as proposals.

Škrty: what gets shorter

Your own agents have already audited dead rules and rule density, and v12.5 acted on it. These five are what this proposal adds to that list.

  • 10 steps to 3

    Evaluation order of a line. Preškolenie, spoje, Vzbura, Výherné žreby, Forsáž, NA EX, Energeťák, Puška, card effects, Výtržník becomes Premeň, Spoj, Odznaky. Badges commute, so their order stops being a rule.

  • 2 exceptions out

    NA EX special cases. “Posilnenie podrží NA EX” goes. “No NA EX on Vzbura” is replaced by two ↻ cancelling. The Odpor shield stays as the single exception.

  • 9 texts shorter

    Cards written in badge language. Vidly “Hore: ↻”. Energeťák “Hore: ×2”. Vínko “Hore: ↻ ×2”. Výherné žreby “Tvoje Hazardy sú ↻”. Silná nezávislá žena “Susední Občania a Starešinovia ×2”. Liberál, Pes, Zrkadlo and Veterán (“NA EX mu dá len ×2”) follow the same pattern.

  • Empty Pitky at once

    Rule: keď ani jeden hráč nemá plný poldeci ani cigarety na fajčenie či barter, zvyšné Pitky kola sa vyhodnotia naraz jedným súhrnom.

    Bots choose skipNaex 13.7 times a game against 5.8 real NA EX. The stratég counted about twenty Pitky without a choice in one turnus; Tempo sits at 5.8.

  • Left alone

    Strach and reči, Chuť and Absťák, the ponuka. The elder layer changed in v12.5, v12.6 and v12.7; it needs playtests, not a fourth redesign in a month. It is still the heaviest part of the rules and the part least connected to the line. If a later pass wants a big cut, that is where it is.

Laboratórium

Build any line from the published set and the seven proposed cards. Click a card to change its variant, give it NA EX or slide a Nástroj under it. The line is evaluated by the real core on every change.

What I would do first

  1. K1 in the client only. Two badges on cards and in the náhľad, no rule change. One AI turnus tells you whether Zrozumiteľnosť and Hĺbka vs. zložitosť move. This is the highest return for the least risk.

  2. K4 and K3 as a rozpracovaná sada for Zrejúca. Mirror six to eight Posilnenie cards, re-price the four Spoločník pečate. Run tools/bot/komba.ts before and after; watch Forsáž per battle and the score gap.

  3. K2 with Družba and Harmonikár. Štrng alone is safe but quiet; with two cards that key off it you can see whether players chase the third pair. Verdict by the next playtest, as with any návrh.

  4. The rest of the cards and the two NA EX exceptions. Only after the first three have a verdict.
